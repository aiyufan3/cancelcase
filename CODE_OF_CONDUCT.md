# Code of Conduct

CancelCase follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree about a change.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not on the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, and no publishing of anyone's private information.
- Do not post credentials, personal data or real account identifiers in issues or pull requests.

This summary does not replace the Covenant; the full text is the standard.

## Reporting

- For a concern you are comfortable raising in public, open a GitHub issue.
- For anything that should stay private, use the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) ("Report a vulnerability" on GitHub) and say that it is a conduct report.

Maintainers review every report, keep the reporter's identity private and respond according to the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
